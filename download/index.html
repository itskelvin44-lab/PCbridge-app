Connected in six steps

From install to typing in under a minute.

1

Install and open on your PC

Install the .deb, then find PC Bridge in your applications menu and open it.

2

Note the session code

The PC shows a 4-digit session code and waits for your phone. Keep this window open.

3

Open the app on your phone

Launch PCBridge from your app list. It starts scanning for nearby devices straight away.

4

Tap the green PCBridge

Your computer appears in green under Nearby devices. Tap it to connect.

5

Type the code

Enter the 4 digits from your PC screen and tap Connect.

6

You’re connected

The status turns green and shows signal and link health. Choose Open keyboard & mouse or Open gamepad.

Three ways to control your PC

Turn your phone sideways and pick a layout.

Questions

 

Do I need to pair over Bluetooth first?

No. The phone app finds the PC itself. Just open both apps.

Does it need Wi-Fi or internet?

No. It uses Bluetooth Low Energy directly between the devices.

What do I need?

A Linux PC with Bluetooth and an Android phone. A Windows version is planned.

How do I install the Android app?

Download the .apk, open it and allow installs from your browser or file manager when asked.
